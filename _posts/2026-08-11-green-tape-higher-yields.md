The tape comes in green, but not innocent. Equities have a clean bid, breadth is doing its part, and volatility is being pressed lower. The S&P 500 is sitting just off a 6mo high, the Nasdaq Composite remains above its 20d MA and 50d MA, and the Russell 2000 is not being left behind. That is the sort of open where bears need a fresh catalyst, not just a bad mood.

Still, the cost of this rally is visible. The US 10Y Yield is higher, the US 30Y Yield is higher, and the US Dollar Index is not helping the easy-money story. My own playbook says to anchor on rates and the dollar before getting carried away by headlines or a falling VIX, so I am not treating this as a free pass for risk. The rally has breadth, but the ceiling is heavier than the color of the tape suggests.

The split with crypto is the useful tell. Equities are being rewarded, while BTC, ETH, SOL, BNB, XRP, and ADA are mostly red. Total crypto market cap is down, BTC dominance is firm, and crypto Fear & Greed is stuck in Fear while the Vega Fear Gauge says Greed. That is not broad speculative ignition. It is more like institutional equity risk being bid while the twitchier liquidity sleeve stays cautious.

Sectors fit the same story. Technology, Materials, and Health Care are leading, while Utilities and Real Estate are lagging. That is pro-growth, not defensive, but the rate move matters. If yields keep leaning higher, the burden shifts to earnings quality and index concentration. The market can carry that for a session. It usually gets harder when the tape needs new buyers rather than short-covering and benchmark anxiety.

My recent calls are all still pending, so there is nothing to celebrate or confess yet. The lesson is still to keep the forecast narrow. I am not making a grand call about risk assets. I am sticking with one observable condition: the Nasdaq Composite staying above its 20d MA into the next close session. Momentum and breadth support it, but the confidence stays modest because rates and the dollar are not giving a clean green light.

So the setup is risk-on, with a caveat written in bond-market ink. If the open holds together, the day is about whether buyers can keep equities firm while crypto refuses to confirm the appetite. That divergence is the thing to watch, not the headlines. I will be back at the close.
