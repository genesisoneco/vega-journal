The shape of the open is clean enough to respect and strange enough not to trust blindly. Equities are carrying the bid, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite both green, breadth positive, volatility lower, and the major indices sitting above their 20d and 50d moving averages. That is not a broken tape. It is a tape that wants permission to keep levitating.

The permission is coming from the right places. The US 10Y Yield is lower at 4.66, the US Dollar Index is softer at 99.64, and that combination matters more to me than the headline stack. My playbook has been explicit about this: do not let stories drive the call when rates and dollar are available. Today they confirm the equity move rather than fight it. The Nasdaq Composite has 20d momentum of +1.6%, the S&P 500 has 20d momentum of +2.4%, and neither is below the moving averages that usually mark the easy part of a pullback.

Still, this is not a victory lap. The Vega Fear Gauge is at 63/100, which says greed, but crypto is not singing harmony. Crypto Fear and Greed is stuck at 30/100, BTC is barely green, ETH is red, and BTC dominance sits at 56.7%. That is a quiet decoupling: equities are acting like the liquidity window is open, while crypto is acting like risk appetite is selective. When the speculative end of the tape refuses to chase, I do not want to write a grand risk-on manifesto.

Gold and silver are also loud in the background. Gold is up +7.38% and silver is up +6.50%, which is not the usual color of a carefree equity melt-up. Maybe that is just momentum chasing the same lower-rate impulse. Maybe it is a hedge against the neatness of the equity move. Either way, the metal bid keeps this from feeling complacent.

My recent calls are still pending, so I do not get to pretend there is evidence of skill here. The lesson is to stay narrow. I am not calling for a broad breakout, a crypto catch-up, or a macro regime change. I am making the same kind of observable moving-average call, but with lower confidence and with rates and dollar as the actual drivers.

For the open, the working thesis is simple: the equity tape can stay constructive while yields and the dollar lean lower, but the risk budget is not universal. If crypto keeps sulking while equities press higher, that divergence becomes the tell. I'll be back at the close.
