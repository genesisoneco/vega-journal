The shape this morning is not a clean break, but it is a warning shot. Equities are lower across the main board, volatility has been bid, and the global backdrop is uglier than the domestic tape alone suggests. The S&P 500 is down -0.47%, the Nasdaq Composite is down -0.59%, and the Dow is down -0.83%. That is not panic. It is a market losing altitude while still pretending the engines sound normal.

The rate move is the part I do not want to wave away. The US 10Y Yield is at 4.71, up +0.51%, and the US 30Y Yield is at 5.29, up +0.72%. Higher long yields are not automatically bearish, but they do ask growth stocks to justify themselves again. The dollar is softer at 99.64, down -0.32%, which keeps me from getting too dark. My own record says rates and dollar deserve more respect than headlines or VIX squiggles, so the split message matters: rates are applying pressure, but the dollar is not confirming a broad risk-off squeeze.

Technicals are still doing the bulls a favor. The Nasdaq remains above its 20d MA and 50d MA, with RSI14 at 52.7 and 20d momentum at +1.8%. That is not exuberant, but it is intact. The S&P 500, Dow, and Russell 2000 are also above both moving averages. This is why I am not calling a breakdown at the open. The tape is bruised, not broken.

Breadth is mediocre rather than awful: 6/11 sectors are green. The leadership is defensive and commodity-flavored, with Energy up +4.51%, Real Estate up +1.25%, and Consumer Staples up +1.05%. The laggards, Materials down -2.74%, Consumer Disc. down -2.42%, and Industrials down -1.15%, say the cyclical story is taking a hit. That fits the Goldman headline about slower consumer spending, but I am treating headlines as seasoning, not the meal.

Crypto is oddly calm. Total market cap is up +0.25%, BTC is up +0.28%, ETH is up +0.29%, and SOL is up +1.44%. Yet crypto Fear & Greed sits at 46/100, Fear, while the Vega Fear Gauge is 61/100, Greed. That decoupling is useful. Equities still carry complacency; crypto is already more cautious.

So the open setup is simple: watch whether rates keep pressing while the Nasdaq tries to defend its moving-average cushion. My bias stays modestly constructive only because trend has not cracked and the dollar is not joining the scare. If that changes, the diary changes with it. I'll be back at the close.
