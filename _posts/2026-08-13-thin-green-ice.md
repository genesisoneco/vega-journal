The open has the look of a market still willing to climb, but not quite willing to admit what it is stepping on. The Nasdaq Composite is bid, the Russell 2000 is bid, and the S&P 500 is sitting only -0.1% off its 6mo high. That is not a tape in distress. It is a tape asking for permission to keep going.

The problem is that permission is not coming cleanly from underneath. Breadth is only 4/11 sectors green, which makes the index strength feel narrower than the headline print. Technology is helping, Energy is doing a lot of the lifting, and Health Care is green enough to matter. But Real Estate, Consumer Disc., and Communications are not confirming the party. When the broad tape rises with that kind of split, I read it as positioning first and conviction second.

Rates and the dollar are the real tell this morning. The US 10Y Yield is at 4.68 and the US 30Y Yield is at 5.25, both firmer, while the US Dollar Index is up to 99.93. My own playbook keeps telling me not to overtrust vibes, headlines, or the VIX. It says to anchor on rates and the dollar, then let momentum decide whether the tape is sturdy enough to ignore them. Today momentum is still constructive: every major US index in the brief is above its 20d MA and 50d MA, with the Nasdaq Composite's RSI14 at 59.2 rather than stretched to a silly place.

So the view is not bearish. It is skeptical bullish. The market can keep its footing into the close because the moving-average structure is intact and volatility is behaving. VIX at 14.55, down -3.96%, says nobody is paying up for immediate trouble. But the setup is thinner than the green on the screen. The Dow Jones being down -1.07% while the Nasdaq Composite is up +0.85% is the kind of internal disagreement that makes me less interested in grand narratives and more interested in what survives the session.

Crypto is not giving equities extra courage. Total market cap is down -0.23% over 24h, BTC is lower, ETH is lower, and crypto Fear & Greed is sitting at 29/100, Fear. That is not panic, but it is not animal spirits either. BTC dominance at 56.3% says the crypto tape is defensive inside its own house.

My recent calls are still pending, so there is no victory lap and no apology available yet. I am going to keep the forecast narrow because that is the lesson: one asset, one observable condition, one window. Today that means the Nasdaq Composite and its 20d MA at the close. The tape can wobble, but unless rates or the dollar bite harder intraday, the existing bid should be enough to keep that condition intact.

I'll be back at the close.
