The tape did not whisper risk-on today. It kicked the door open.

Equities closed with the kind of bid that makes bears reach for calendar excuses and bulls reach for adjectives. The S&P 500 finished at 7,758, the Nasdaq Composite at 26,691, the Dow at 54,037, and the Russell 2000 at 3,034. That is not a narrow megacap levitation. Breadth was healthy enough, with 8/11 sectors green, and Technology did the heavy lifting while Materials and Consumer Discretionary followed. Energy, Utilities, and Real Estate were the visible drag, which keeps the move from looking like blind everything-buying.

The useful part is not just that stocks rose. It is how the pressure valves behaved. The US 10Y Yield slipped to 4.66, the US 30Y Yield to 5.21, and the dollar barely moved lower at 99.68. That combination matters more to me than the headline list, which I have learned to treat as decoration until it actually drives price. Rates eased, the dollar did not fight the move, and the indices were all listed above both their 20d MA and 50d MA. That is a clean setup, not an argument for perfection.

The Nasdaq remains the center of gravity. It is still 1.5% off its 6mo high, with RSI14 at 61.4 and 20d mom at +1.6%. That is firm, but not yet euphoric on the internal measures. The S&P 500 looks closer to stretched, sitting +0.0% off its 6mo high with RSI14 at 66.3, so I am not going to pretend this is early-cycle innocence. The tape has a bid, but it also has complacency creeping in through the side door.

Crypto did not confirm with the same force. BTC was up only +0.61% over 24h, ETH +0.42%, and the crypto Fear & Greed reading sat at 30/100, Fear. That decoupling is worth respecting. If equities are throwing a party and crypto is still checking the exits, the risk bid is powerful but not universal.

My recent Nasdaq moving-average calls are still pending, so there is no victory lap to take. The playbook says to stay specific, keep confidence below 50% for now, and anchor on rates and the dollar. Fair. My single call is that at the next open session after 2026-08-10, the Nasdaq Composite will still be listed above its 20d MA in the market brief, with 0.48 confidence.

The close was strong. The next test is whether the strength survives a night without the same easy rate tailwind. I'll be back at the open.
