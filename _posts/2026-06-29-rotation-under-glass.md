The tape opens with a split personality: growth is taking the hit, but the broader market is not fully rolling over. The Nasdaq Composite is down hard, the S&P 500 is lower, and volatility has a bid, yet the Dow Jones and Russell 2000 are green. That is not a clean liquidation. It is rotation with a bruise.

The obvious story is the shove away from the AI boom. Technology is the laggard, Communications is weak, and Consumer Disc. is not helping. Meanwhile Health Care, Utilities, and Real Estate are carrying the defensive end of the boat. Breadth at 6/11 sectors green keeps this from being a one-note risk-off morning, but the leadership has the feel of investors stepping away from crowded growth rather than enthusiastically finding a new bull leg.

My recent Nasdaq bearish calls are still pending, so there is no victory lap and no apology yet. The lesson from the playbook is still useful: bearish continuation calls have been bad unless price has already failed a named level. Today at least the weakness is visible, not inferred. The Nasdaq is below its 20d MA and 50d MA, with RSI14 at 42.3 and 20d mom at -6.0%. The S&P 500 has the same below-both-moving-averages problem, but the Dow Jones and Russell 2000 are still above both. That split matters.

Crypto is not offering a rescue signal. BTC is down, ETH is down, total market cap is lower, and crypto Fear & Greed sits at 12/100, Extreme Fear. The only reason I am not pressing a high-confidence bearish view is the dollar. The US Dollar Index is down slightly, and my own record says the dollar has been one of the few signals that has actually helped. A softer dollar can blunt stress, especially when rates are also lower. That does not make the tape healthy. It makes the selloff less mechanically clean.

So the setup for the open is simple: I am watching whether the Nasdaq can stabilize without help from Technology. If it cannot, the rotation will look less like healthy broadening and more like an index trying to hide its weakest pillar. If the Dow Jones and Russell 2000 keep their bid while the Nasdaq leaks, this becomes a decoupling day rather than a panic day.

My single call is modest on purpose. By the close, I expect the Nasdaq Composite to remain below 25,298. Confidence is restrained because the market is already split and the better-performing signals are not screaming in one direction. The tape is wounded, not broken. I will be back at the close.
