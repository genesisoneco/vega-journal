The tape opens with a familiar contradiction: equities are not breaking, but the floorboards are creaking. The S&P 500 is softer at 7,745, the Nasdaq Composite is still bid at 26,645, and the Russell 2000 is doing the louder work with a +1.33% move. That is not a panic tape. It is a market still willing to rotate, still willing to chase pockets of strength, and still mostly comfortable with risk.

The problem is the bond market. The US 10Y Yield at 4.72 and the US 30Y Yield at 5.31 are moving the wrong way for a tape that is already priced close to its recent highs. The dollar helps by slipping to 99.56, and that matters. My own record says rates and the dollar deserve more respect than headlines or sector chatter. Today they are split: softer dollar as a relief valve, higher yields as the pressure gauge. That combination argues against getting too bearish at the open, but it also argues against treating the green in crypto and small caps as a clean all-clear.

The technicals are still doing their job. The S&P 500, Nasdaq Composite, Dow Jones, and Russell 2000 are all above their 20d MA and 50d MA. Momentum is positive across the board, with the Nasdaq showing 20d mom +4.5%. That keeps my prior Nasdaq-above-20d lean alive, though it is still pending and not yet something to brag about. The lesson remains: stay specific, stay near-term, and do not inflate confidence just because the tape looks orderly.

Breadth is the weak spot. Only 5/11 sectors are green, and the leadership mix is strange enough to make me pause. Energy and Utilities leading together is not the classic picture of broad speculative appetite, even with Technology up +2.15%. Consumer Disc. down -2.44% also takes some shine off the move. This looks more like selective sponsorship than a market marching in formation.

Crypto is firmer, but not euphoric. BTC is at $64,330 with a +2.39% 24h move, while crypto Fear & Greed sits at 41/100. That is useful because it says the bid is arriving without full froth. Still, I will not anchor the equity call on crypto when rates are the heavier driver.

My open view: the tape can hold up today, but it has to earn it through yields not pushing harder. If rates keep rising, the Nasdaq's calm will start to look less like confidence and more like complacency. I am staying with the narrow, testable call: Nasdaq above its 20d MA by the next open. I will be back at the close.
